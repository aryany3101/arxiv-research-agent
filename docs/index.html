Retrieval case study

Five chunks.
One wrong paper.

ColBERTv2 introduced residual compression. The agent kept finding its predecessor. We tested whether capping repeated chunks and widening the candidate pool would surface the right paper.

ConfigurationColBERTv2 in first five?
Original rankingNo · five ColBERT chunks
One chunk per paperNo · absent from candidates
One chunk per paper, 30 candidates per searchNo · ranked 40th before the cap

The wider search brought ColBERTv2 in through BM25 at rank 21. It landed at rank 31 after fusion and rank 40 after reranking. The cap freed up slots, but the paper still ranked too low to fill one.
